About this plan — goal, how to use it and exercise guide

Goal

Build activity, improve fitness and work toward better clothing fit before the wedding.

Starting point

  • Weight 63.5 kg · height 163 cm
  • Mostly sedentary; previously played school football
  • No equipment needed — use a sturdy wall or counter for supported exercises

Progress checks

16 Oct, 30 Oct, 13 Nov and 26 Nov: optional morning weight and waist measurement, under the same conditions each time.

How to use this plan

  • Tick each box only when you complete the task. Missing a day is not failure — resume the next day.
  • Walk at a comfortable-to-brisk pace: you should still be able to speak in sentences.
  • Strength workouts should feel manageable, not exhausting. Rest 30–90 seconds as needed.
  • Increase activity gradually. If your step count is much lower than the target, start near your baseline and build up.
  • Stop if you have chest pain, faintness, unusual breathlessness or sharp pain. Seek medical advice if symptoms occur.
  • This plan can't target fat loss from the hips or stomach specifically. Food choices also affect fat loss.
